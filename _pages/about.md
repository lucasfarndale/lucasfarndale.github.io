Hi, I'm Lucas. I'm a postdoctoral research associate at the University of Glasgow and the CRUK Scotland Institute, in [Ke Yuan's group](https://kyuanlab.org/). My [research](/projects/) develops computational methods to find new, clinically useful information in cancer images, using self-supervised learning, spatial statistics and the integration of multiple data types.

I'm currently working on the ORCHESTRATE prostate cancer project, led by Hing Leung, Ed Roberts and Ke Yuan. There I test whether reported spatial relationships between cell types in tumours are statistically robust, and I build the computational analysis of multiplex (CODEX) images of prostate-draining lymph nodes from mouse models.

I recently completed my PhD in Precision Medicine at the University of Glasgow, supervised by [Robert Insall](https://www.beatson.gla.ac.uk/beatson-research/beatson-research-groups/robert-insall-cell-migration-and-chemotaxis.html), Ke Yuan and Ed Roberts. My thesis, *Super Vision Without Supervision: Enhancing Biomedical Image Analysis with Self-Supervised Learning*, is about discovering prognostic biomarkers from high-content imaging, and about distilling knowledge from advanced imaging modalities into models that only need routine H&E slides. Before that I studied Mathematics (MSci, First Class Honours) at Glasgow.

I also teach across Mathematics, Computing Science and Cancer Sciences, and help organise the Glasgow Computational Biology community and its conference (see [outreach and service](/outreach/)).
