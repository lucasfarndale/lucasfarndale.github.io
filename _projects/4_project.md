Spatial relationships between cell types in tumours are widely reported. As part of the ORCHESTRATE prostate cancer project, I test whether these reported relationships are statistically robust, using CosMx spatial transcriptomics data (about 498,000 cells from 89 patients), four published imaging cohorts, and a screen of 215 papers.

This work involves designing simulation and calibration pipelines, and I have released a tested, installable software package for the analysis.

I am also building the computational analysis of CODEX multiplex images of prostate-draining lymph nodes from mouse models (about 1.7 million cells): segmentation, vessel morphometry, spatial statistics with density-controlled nulls, quality control, and batch assessment.
